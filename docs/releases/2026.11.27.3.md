drivers. Unmarked drivers need names; numeric fields stay hidden, disabled and

dynamic editor. Use consistent compact cards for initial and added driver rows.

drivers and retain supplier eligibility/per-person fee calculations. Partner
changes affecting the accepted fee still require returning to recalculate.

rewrite of saved bookings. This release includes the preceding .2 empty-editor
and compact-offer-form corrections as well.
