RentalCar Backups — Terms of Use

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Private business utility

RentalCar Backups is maintained by Idan, operator of Poland Rent a Car, for authorized internal backup and recovery work. It is not a public backup subscription, rental contract or invitation for customers to provide Google account access.

Authorized use

Use the utility only with accounts, data and storage you are authorized to manage. Google authorization permits the requested backup operations; it does not transfer ownership of account data. Do not share tokens, encryption keys or backup archives publicly. Read the Privacy Policy before granting access.

Operational responsibilities

The operator must verify completed uploads, maintain adequate storage, keep recovery keys separately, monitor failures and test restoration. A created archive or successful upload alone does not prove that a complete recovery will succeed. Keep the authoritative working system and existing backups until migration and recovery checks are complete.

Current availability

Independent encrypted Google Drive backups are being configured. No uninterrupted availability, completed backup schedule or successful recovery is represented by these pages. Google Drive and Render remain subject to their own terms, account limits and availability.

Stopping use and contact

The operator can stop the job and revoke Google account authorization. Existing copies must be deleted separately as described in the Privacy Policy. Questions and access or deletion requests: idan.poland@gmail.com.
